Website privacy notice

Minds Digital Legacy (MDL) · Rwanda

This notice explains how MDL uses information submitted through the course registration, technology service, corporate training and internship forms on this website. It is an initial website notice, not legal advice or a substitute for a complete organizational data-protection program.

Information we collect

Depending on the form, we may collect your name, contact details, organization or institution, learning or service interests, internship application information, and your consent. Do not send passwords, payment PINs, health information or other sensitive personal information in these forms. The internship form does not accept CV or school-letter uploads.

Why we use it

MDL uses submitted information to respond to enquiries, discuss training and services, assess internship applications, and administer a resulting request. Submitting an internship application does not guarantee placement. Academic placements may require institutional approval, and placements depend on open opportunities, eligibility and supervision capacity.

Sharing, access and retention

Information is stored in MDL's application database and should be accessible only to authorized personnel who need it to handle the request. MDL should not sell submitted personal information. The website operator must establish appropriate retention periods, access controls, backups and processor agreements before collecting real applicants' information.

Your choices and contact

You may contact MDL to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal requirements. Add MDL's verified contact details here before launch so people can exercise these rights and ask privacy questions.

Children and student applicants

High-school applicants should involve a parent or guardian where required and apply through their school when an academic placement is needed. The current form records guardian involvement but does not independently verify identity or accept a school placement letter. MDL should confirm a suitable supervised process before accepting applications from minors.

Before public launch, MDL must replace the contact placeholder with a monitored contact address, confirm its lawful basis and retention schedule, and review this notice against Rwanda's current data-protection requirements.
